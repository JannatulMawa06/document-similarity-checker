Solar power is one of the fastest growing forms of renewable energy in the world. Solar panels capture sunlight and convert it directly into electricity using photovoltaic cells. Because sunlight is free and widely available, solar power can be installed almost anywhere, from rooftops to large open fields. As solar panel technology improves and manufacturing costs continue to fall, more homeowners are installing solar systems to lower their electricity bills. Solar energy also produces no direct emissions while generating power, making it an important part of efforts to fight climate change and move away from fossil fuels.
