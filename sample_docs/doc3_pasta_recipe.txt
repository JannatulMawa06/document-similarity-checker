Making a simple pasta dish at home only takes a few basic ingredients. Start by boiling water in a large pot and adding a generous amount of salt. Cook the pasta until it is tender but still slightly firm, then drain it and set it aside. While the pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a pan and add minced garlic until it becomes fragrant. Toss the cooked pasta into the pan along with some grated parmesan cheese and fresh basil leaves. Season with salt and pepper to taste. This quick recipe is perfect for a weeknight dinner when you do not have much time to cook.
